16.   Module              This course is an introductory course to Economics and covers both
      Description,        microeconomics and macroeconomics. The course also introduces
      Purpose      and    students to economics and makes them appreciate the functions of
      Aim(s)/             the economy as a whole thereby enhancing their participation in
      Objective(s)        economic decision making. It is designed to introduce key economics
                          concepts and techniques applicable as tools for rational economic
                          decision-making.
                          Aim(s)/ Objective(s)
                          To equip students with:
                                   How to describe the nature of the economic problem
                                     and the categories of scarce resources
                                   Differentiating between the economic systems in the
                                     world.
                                   Understanding the theories of demand and supply
                                   Appreciate the costs of production and market
                                     structures.
                                   Describing government intervention and market failure.
                                Understanding the macroeconomic side of economics
                                 like aggregate demand, aggregate supply, inflation, and
                                 unemployment, fiscal and monetary policies.
                              Application of the economic perspective and to reason
                                 accurately and objectively about economic matters.
17.   Module          Upon completion of this module, students will be able to:
      Learning                Understand the Economic problem of allocation and
      Outcomes                   distribution of resources.
                              Analyze factors that determine demand and supply
                              Understand the concept of elasticity of demand and supply
                              Evaluate effect of market structure on firm behavior
                              Analyze concept of derived demand for labor and capital
                              Analyse existence of impact of externalities
                              Understand mechanism of Income distribution.
