=== Slide 9 ===
         Islamic Aqeedah
   Understanding the purpose of creation in a complete sense is beyond
    man's innate abilities. Man is a finite created being and can not
    reasonably hope to fully comprehend the actions of the infinite Creator.
    Hence, God made it a part of man's nature to worship Him, and He sent
    prophets and books of divine revelation to clarify the aspect of the
    purpose of creation which was within man's mental ability to grasp. That

    and the main message of the prophets was to worship God alone,
   Tawheed al-'Ebaadah. Consequently, the gravest sin is Shirk, the worship
